Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about direct farmer-buyer trade and AI pricing.

By connecting restaurants, retailers, and wholesalers directly with verified farmers, produce moves straight from the farm gate to the buyer. We do not extract broker fees, allowing farmers to earn up to 35% more while buyers save 20-25% on purchase expenses.

Our price service uses transparent heuristics combining reference mandi baselines, harvest freshness age (recent harvests earn a quality premium), volume scales (bulk wholesale batches receive volume efficiency scaling), and regional demand. It is strictly advisory: farmers always retain full freedom to set their desired listing prices.

In this MVP demonstration, orders are placed with Cash on Delivery (COD) / Direct Settlement upon produce inspection. We do not store card details or charge online processing cuts. Buyers settle directly with farmers.

Surplus Rescue alerts farmers when perishable produce (like tomatoes, greens, or ripe bananas) is approaching optimal shelf life with unsold quantity. Farmers can apply an advisory 15-30% discount to feature the lot on the "Surplus Deals" section, allowing food businesses to procure at deep discounts while preventing agricultural waste.
