Our Mission

Fresh from the Farm. Fair for Everyone.

Transforming agricultural trade from an opaque, fragmented broker network into an open, equitable digital marketplace.

The Problem

Smallholder and progressive farmers across India produce world-class agricultural yields, but have historically been price-takers at physical mandis. Information asymmetry, unstandardized commission cuts, transport cartels, and multi-layered intermediaries diminish agricultural incomes while burdening food businesses and consumers with inflated food costs.

Our Core Pillars

AI Price Assistance

Transparent multi-factor benchmarks factoring in freshness, volume scales, and mandi references. 100% advisory.

Surplus Rescue

Proactively detecting inventory at risk of spoilage and connecting it with budget-conscious buyers before waste occurs.

Direct Settlement

Safe, direct trade between verified producers and commercial kitchens with zero platform commission deductions.

Hackathon MVP Disclosure

This system was designed and constructed for a 24-hour hackathon demonstrating production-ready full-stack architecture, clean database persistence, authentic Indian agricultural workflows, and explainable AI services.
