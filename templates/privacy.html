Privacy & Data Ethics Policy

Effective: October 2026

1. Data Storage & Security

All passwords stored in FarmDirect AI are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 / SHA-256 via Werkzeug. Plaintext passwords are never stored, logged, or exposed in administrative dashboards.

2. Counterparty Visibility

Farmer and buyer phone numbers and locations are shared exclusively with the counterparty of a placed order to coordinate direct dispatch and settlement.

3. Synthetic Benchmark Datasets

Historical pricing benchmarks used by the pricing assistant are synthetic reference baselines designed for demonstration, ensuring complete transparency without claiming proprietary closed data.
