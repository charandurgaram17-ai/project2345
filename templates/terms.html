Terms of Direct Trade

Effective: October 2026 • Hackathon Version 1.0

1. Nature of Platform

FarmDirect AI is a peer-to-peer technology facilitator connecting agricultural farmers directly with buyers. The platform does not hold custody of produce, operate freight fleets, or collect middleman brokerage commissions.

2. Advisory Pricing

All recommendations generated by the AI Fair Price Assistant are purely heuristic benchmarks for informational decision support. Farmers retain sole legal authority over their final asking prices.

3. Direct Settlement

Payment arrangements (Cash on Delivery or agreed commercial terms) are settled directly between buyer and farmer upon inspection.

4. Listing Integrity

Farmers agree to provide accurate stock quantities, harvest dates, and condition descriptions. Administration reserves the right to disable misleading listings.
