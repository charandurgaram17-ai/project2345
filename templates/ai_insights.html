Feature 3: AI Surplus Rescue Architecture
Food Waste Prevention
The Challenge: Post-Harvest Spoilage

In traditional supply chains, over 25% of fresh produce spoils in transit or waiting at mandis. Farmers bear 100% of this loss.

Our Solution: Proactive Surplus Discovery

The Surplus Rescue module monitors inventory age and remaining shelf life. When inventory risk is flagged, it suggests an advisory clearance discount (15% to 30%). Farmers can apply this with 1 click to feature immediately on the marketplace's "Surplus Deals" channel.
