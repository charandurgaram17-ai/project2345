The Problem We Solve

Why Agriculture Needs Direct Digital Linkages

In typical agricultural marketing across India, produce passes through 4 to 6 intermediaries: local village aggregators, Commission Agents (arhtiyas), APMC mandi wholesalers, sub-wholesalers, and neighborhood distributors.

Each tier adds transport delays, holding wastage, and 10-15% profit margins. By the time produce reaches a restaurant or retail shelf, the consumer pays 300% more, while the farmer who grew it earned barely 30% of the value.

FarmDirect AI Solution: Direct transparent connection, AI price guidance, verified direct contact, and atomic inventory management.
