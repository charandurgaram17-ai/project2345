How FarmDirect AI Connects You

A transparent, direct three-step bridge between producers and commercial purchasers.

1. Farmer Lists Produce

Farmers list their lot with harvest dates, quantity, and photos. The AI Price Assistant provides transparent mandi benchmark suggestions to maximize fair farmer income.

2. Smart Matching & Order

Restaurants, wholesalers, and grocery stores find optimal lots matched to their quantity, distance, and freshness specs. Instant atomic order reservation prevents overselling.

3. Direct Dispatch & Settlement

The farmer accepts the order directly. Dispatch is coordinated with transparent buyer contact details, and settlement occurs directly via Cash on Delivery or agreed terms with zero platform cuts.
